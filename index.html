About Mojo Brookzz

Dyon "Mojo" Brooks is an actor, writer, producer, and nationally acclaimed comedian starring in Tyler Perry's Netflix series Miss Governor. He recently completed a 50+ city sold-out national tour, I Know You F*ckin Lyin, and sold out the Chicago Theatre in his hometown.

A hand-picked standout on the arena-level We Them Ones Tour with Mike Epps, Mojo is preparing to rejoin the tour for the third time, while launching an international tour and an upcoming stand-up special. A breakout from Wild 'N Out Season 21, he was personally selected by Nick Cannon after viral sketches earned 28+ million likes and built 4.5+ million followers across platforms.

Born and raised in Chicago, Mojo has partnered with the Chicago Bears on Emmy-winning campaigns and remains deeply committed to youth development and community impact.
